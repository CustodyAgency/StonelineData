Why Stoneline

Custody means accountability,
not just storage

Anyone can sell you disk space. Stoneline Data gives you a named operator, a signed agreement, documented uptime, and a written report every month confirming your data is intact.

Encrypted at rest and in transit

Your data is encrypted before it leaves your hands. We handle the keys, the process, and the documentation to prove it.

Multiple geographic locations

Your data lives in more than one place at all times. A single outage, fire, or regional event cannot touch your backup.

Written monthly confirmation

Every month you receive a plain-language report confirming your sync status, storage usage, and integrity check results.

Signed custody agreement

We put our commitments in writing before we touch your data. Scope, retention, encryption standard, and liability are all defined up front.

HIPAA and compliance ready

Medical and legal clients get a signed Business Associate Agreement and the documented security practices required for regulated data.

A real person is responsible

Stoneline is operator-managed. You know who to call. There is no ticket queue standing between you and the person who holds your data.
