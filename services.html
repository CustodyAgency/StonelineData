FAQ

Common questions

What is the onboarding process?
After you request a quote, we confirm your storage volume and compliance needs, then provision dedicated cloud storage accounts in your name. You receive a signed custody agreement before any data migration begins. Onboarding typically takes two to five business days depending on plan tier.
Can I upgrade my plan later?
Yes. Storage tiers and feature levels can be adjusted as your needs grow. We will provision additional capacity or add compliance features and update your custody agreement accordingly. Upgrades are prorated monthly.
What happens to my data if I cancel?
We export your data to a format you can access directly, deliver it via secure S3 presigned URL or a physical drive at your request, and provide written confirmation of delivery. Your buckets and all access credentials are then deleted after a 30-day hold period.
How are creators and photographers billed differently?
Creators with large archives often use a hybrid of an active plan for current work and cold archive pricing for completed projects. This keeps monthly costs manageable as your library grows. We size the tiers together during onboarding.
Do you provide a BAA for HIPAA clients automatically?
Yes. Any client whose data includes Protected Health Information receives a signed Business Associate Agreement before the account is provisioned. You will not be asked to store PHI without a BAA in place.
